FROM THE FIRST CONVERSATION

A clear route
to your next ride.

Work through the arrangements in order, so the important details are agreed before pickup.

Have these details ready.

  • Your pickup address and destination entrance.
  • Your wheelchair type, dimensions, and boarding preference.
  • Your companion count and return pickup plan.

Keep a contact number handy if the timing changes.

  1. Share the journey

    Provide the pickup, destination, date, time, and passenger count. Mention the return journey at the start.

  2. Check the fit

    Discuss your mobility device, boarding equipment, securement, companions, and preferred assistance.

  3. Confirm the arrangement

    Agree availability, pricing, contact details, and cancellation terms directly with the provider.

BEFORE YOU SET OFF

Talk through the details.

Open a question to see what to discuss when arranging your journey.

Can I arrange a return pickup?

Share the expected finish time and discuss a fixed pickup or another agreed arrangement. Ask how changes, waiting time, and extra charges are handled.

What if I don’t know whether I need a ramp or lift?

Choose “Not sure” in the booking form and describe your device. The provider should explain the available equipment and confirm compatibility.

What should I check for an airport journey?

Confirm the terminal, meeting point, flight details, luggage space, and companion seating. Ask what happens if your flight is delayed.

Does this include emergency or medical treatment?

This website does not arrange emergency response or medical care during travel. Discuss medical-support requirements separately with the appropriate care provider.
