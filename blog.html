Before your first accessible ride

Start with the questions that help you discuss the right vehicle, boarding equipment, and support.

Read the full guide
Quick overview

Share your mobility device type, dimensions, and weight information. Ask the provider to confirm vehicle suitability, ramp or lift requirements, and securement compatibility. Include companions and luggage in the discussion. Agree pickup details, pricing, and cancellation terms before booking.

AppointmentsGUIDE 02

Plan the return before you leave

A visit can run longer than expected. Talk through the journey home while arranging the pickup.

Read the guide

Discuss whether the return time is fixed or agreed after the appointment. Confirm the meeting point and keep a contact number ready. Ask how waiting time, delays, and changed pickup arrangements affect availability and charges.

CompanionsGUIDE 03

When a caregiver comes along

Keep the passenger involved while making practical arrangements for everyone travelling.

Read the guide

Include all travellers in the passenger count. Ask about seating, luggage space, and the assistance available during boarding. Agree who receives journey information and who can be contacted if plans change. The passenger’s own preferences should guide the conversation.

Plan your journey
PlanningGUIDE 04

Ramp or lift: what to ask

Equipment suitability depends on the device and vehicle. Begin with the details of your wheelchair or scooter.

Read the guide

Describe your mobility device and provide its dimensions and weight information. Ask about the equipment’s operating limits, securement arrangements, and available assistance. If you are unsure what you need, say so; suitability must be confirmed directly before the ride is agreed.

Plan your journey
AirportsGUIDE 05

Make the airport meeting point clear

Flight details, luggage, and a precise pickup location help bring the travel plan together.

Read the guide

Share the flight number, terminal, arrival or departure time, and companion count. Confirm where to meet and how to contact the provider. Discuss luggage space and ask what happens if a flight is delayed or the meeting point changes.

Plan your journey
AppointmentsGUIDE 06

Keep recurring journeys organized

Bring repeated appointment dates into one conversation without assuming each ride is already confirmed.

Read the guide

Keep a list of dates, destinations, appointment times, and return requirements. Discuss how each journey is confirmed, when payment is due, and what notice is needed for changes. Recheck boarding and companion needs whenever they change.
