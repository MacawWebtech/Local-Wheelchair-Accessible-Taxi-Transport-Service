Start with
your journey.

Share your route, mobility device, and preferred boarding assistance before discussing availability with the provider.

  1. Prepare your route

    Note pickup, destination, date, and time. Include the return pickup arrangement and any appointment entrance details.

  2. Describe your access needs

    Include your wheelchair or scooter, its dimensions if known, and whether you need a ramp or lift. Share your preferred boarding assistance.

  3. Confirm the arrangements

    Discuss vehicle suitability, the complete fare, and pickup details before travelling. Keep the provider’s contact number available on the day.
