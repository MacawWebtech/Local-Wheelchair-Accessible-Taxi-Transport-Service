AVOID SURPRISES ON THE DAY

Ask about
additional charges.

Not every charge applies to every ride. Agree which terms apply to your journey before booking.

Explain any planned stops, appointment waiting time, and the return pickup before the price is agreed. Changes to these details can affect the quote.

Ask which charges are included, which could be added, and how the provider will tell you about a change. Keep the cancellation terms with your journey details.

Explore appointment-travel fare questions

  • Waiting time

    Check any included allowance, extra charges, and whether waiting is available.

  • Changes or cancellation

    Ask about notice periods, rescheduling, and fees if your plans change.

  • Extra stops or a longer route

    Discuss planned stops and how a changed destination affects the agreed fare.

  • Airport or parking costs

    Confirm whether parking, pickup-area charges, or other location costs are included.

PRICING QUESTIONS

Know what you’re agreeing to.

Can I get an instant fare online?

A live route calculator and pricing service are not connected. Prepare the journey details and request a confirmed quote directly from the provider.

Does a round-trip quote include waiting?

Not automatically. Confirm whether both journeys and any waiting allowance are included, and ask about charges if your appointment runs late.

Are recurring journeys priced differently?

Discuss the complete schedule with the provider. Agree the fare and confirmation process for each date, including any terms for changed or cancelled appointments.

When do I pay?

Ask the provider about accepted payment methods and when payment is due. No payment is collected through this website; Stripe and PayPal are not connected.

What information helps with an accurate quote?

Share the pickup, destination, date, time, passenger count, mobility-device details, boarding requirements, and return arrangements.
