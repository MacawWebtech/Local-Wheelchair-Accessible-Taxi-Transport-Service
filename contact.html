BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few details that help.

What information should I have ready?

Bring the pickup and destination addresses, date, arrival time, device details, passenger count, and return requirements.

Can someone contact you on my behalf?

A caregiver or companion can help organize the conversation. Agree who receives updates while keeping the passenger involved in decisions about assistance.

Can I discuss a recurring schedule?

Keep a list of dates, appointment times, and return journeys. Ask the provider to confirm each arrangement and the terms for any changes.
