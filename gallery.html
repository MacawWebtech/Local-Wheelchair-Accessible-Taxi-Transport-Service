YOUR DEVICE COMES FIRST

A photograph
can only show so much.

The assigned vehicle should be checked against your own device and travel requirements. A photograph can show equipment, but it cannot confirm dimensions, operating limits, or availability.

Have your device information ready, explain the assistance you prefer, and ask the provider to confirm the arrangement before agreeing the journey.

Provide your wheelchair or scooter dimensions and weight information. Ask the provider to check the assigned vehicle, equipment limits, and securement compatibility.

  • Discuss the assistance you prefer when boarding.
  • Include companions and luggage in the space requirements.
  • Confirm whether you can remain in your wheelchair during travel.
  • Ask about driver training and the checks made before departure.

COMMON EQUIPMENT QUESTIONS

Plan around your own needs.

Can I choose the vehicle shown?

The photographs illustrate equipment examples. They do not show confirmed availability or an assigned vehicle. Discuss your requirements with the provider before accepting a ride.

Will my powered wheelchair or scooter fit?

Share the device type, dimensions, and weight information. The provider must confirm equipment limits, securement compatibility, and the available passenger space.

How much room is there for a companion?

Include every traveller in the passenger count. Confirm seating and luggage capacity for the specific vehicle assigned to your journey.
